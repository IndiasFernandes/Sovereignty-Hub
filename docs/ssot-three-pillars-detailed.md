# SOURCE OF TRUTH: The Three Pillars (Detailed)

> **This document extracts and structures the three pillars from the official Investment Proposal. It cannot be changed unless explicitly requested. All website content must reflect these pillars correctly.**

---

## Pillar 1: Budget and Policy Intelligence

**Core purpose**: Improve financing and policy decision-making.

**What is being built**: Analytical tools and frameworks to assess current allocation, identify inefficiencies, cost policy options, and support realistic budget decisions.

**Key deliverables (12 months)**:
- Budget Review Reports (2–3 pilot countries)
- Resource Mapping Analyses
- Policy Costing Models
- Spending Alignment Recommendations
- Legislative and Budget Impact Notes

**Decision value**: Helps governments understand where money is going, where gaps and inefficiencies exist, and which policy choices are financially realistic.

**Why investable**: Strengthens national capacity to manage and optimize domestic health financing; reduces inefficiencies; prioritizes high-impact interventions; makes realistic policy commitments in donor transition context; links financial analysis to parliamentary processes.

---

## Pillar 2: Operational Intelligence and Health Security

**Core purpose**: Strengthen planning, forecasting, and continuity of care.

**What is being built**: Lightweight decision-support system integrating financial, epidemiological, and operational data through dashboards, analytical models, forecasting tools, and coordination protocols.

**Key deliverables (12 months)**:
- Interactive Dashboards
- Burden vs. Spending Analysis Reports
- Forecasting Models
- Decision Briefs
- Health Security Coordination Protocols
- Data Governance and Indicator Framework

**Decision value**: Helps decision-makers track system performance, anticipate risks, improve procurement and planning, and coordinate responses during routine operations and disruption.

**AI application** (selective, governed):
- Forecasting resource needs (drugs, diagnostics, service demand)
- Identifying inefficiencies in spending relative to disease burden
- Synthesizing multi-source data into structured decision outputs

**Why investable**: Strengthens national capacity to monitor system performance, anticipate operational risks, make coordinated resource decisions; provides practical tools for planning, procurement, continuity of care; avoids complex/costly infrastructure.

---

## Pillar 3: Parliamentary and Public Accountability

**Core purpose**: Ensure decisions are debated, tracked, and followed through.

**What is being built**: Structured accountability layer linking financing decisions to implementation, oversight, and stakeholder dialogue.

**Key deliverables (12 months)**:
- Caucus Engagement Frameworks
- Budget Advocacy Toolkit
- Oversight and Monitoring Framework
- Accountability Scorecards / Tracking Tools
- Structured Dialogue Mechanisms
- Community-informed Feedback Channels

**Decision value**: Converts evidence into political ownership, implementation follow-through, and stronger transparency.

**Why investable**: Increases transparency and accountability in use of public resources; strengthens link between allocation and implementation; contributes to public trust; essential in constrained financing and demand for efficiency.

---

## Cross-Cutting Function: Governance and Regional Coordination

**Core purpose**: Integrate the three pillars into one operating mechanism.

**What is being built**: Lean coordination and delivery structure connecting partner alignment, quarterly review, pilot-country engagement, disruption response, and sustainability planning.

**Key deliverables**:
- Operational Governance Structure
- Regional Partner Network Map
- Quarterly Review Reports
- Rapid Response / Coordination Protocol
- Sustainability and Scale Roadmap
