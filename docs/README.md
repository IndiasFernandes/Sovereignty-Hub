### Rule

**All website content** (`index.html`, modals, any future pages) **must align clearly** with these documents. Before editing the site, review the SSOT to ensure correct:

- Hub name (EECA **Lung** Health Sovereignty Hub)
- Three pillars (Budget and Policy Intelligence; Operational Intelligence and Health Security; Parliamentary and Public Accountability)
- Four opportunity dimensions (Global Fund Transition; Lung Health Crises; Geopolitical Instability; Inefficient Resource Allocation)
- Governance (Regional Steering Committee, Parliamentary Council, Civil Society Council, Technical Secretariat)
- Budget ($1M MVP) and target audience (national donors, politicians)
- Global TB Caucus network (9 national caucuses)
- Primary Users: Ministries of Health and Finance; Parliamentarians and oversight bodies; Technical and implementing partners
- Core Team: Gayané Arustamyan (Director), Alesia Matusevich (Public Health Specialist), Daniel Fernandes (AI & Systems Architecture Lead)
